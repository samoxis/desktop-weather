## Visual acceptance target

Natural photographic materials, believable full-sized rural buildings and vegetation, neutral lighting, restrained saturation, a circular inhabited Romanian village integrated into surrounding countryside. Tractor geometry and materials must match a real vehicle rather than a simplified toy. Authentic tire silhouettes and tread; consistent driver/cab scale. Waterwheel on a fixed shaft; paddles meet water. Proper ground contact, shadows and orientation through a complete tractor turn. Actual flowing water and rain impacts. Animals must be anatomically credible throughout movement.

Replacing the scene with a still image alone would not meet the animation requirements. A photographic illustration and a mechanically correct but simplified WebGL scene each solve only part of the request. Detailed models, independently rigged moving parts, material maps and matching light are needed together. Visual review of movement remains necessary after mechanical tests pass.
